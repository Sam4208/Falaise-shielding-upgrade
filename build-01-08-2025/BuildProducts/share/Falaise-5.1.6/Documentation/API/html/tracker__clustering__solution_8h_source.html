31  /// \brief A collection of tracker clusters
32  /**
33  * A tracker clustering solution stores the possible result computed by a
34  * clustering algorithm from an original collection of tracker calibrated hits.
35  *
36  * Original collection of Geiger hits (handled by a 'calibrated_data' object):
37  *
38  * +------------------------+
39  * | o |
40  * | oo oo |
41  * | ooo ooooo |
42  * | ooooooo |
43  * | o o |
44  * | oo o |
45  * | oo |
46  * | o |
47  * +------------------------+
48  *
49  * A possible collection of clusters built from the source:
50  *
51  * +------------------------+
52  * | 1 | Legend: 1 : hits belonging to cluster #1
53  * | 11 11 | 0 : hits belonging to cluster #2
54  * | 111 11111 | U : unclustered hits
55  * | 1111111 |
56  * | 0 U |
57  * | 00 U |
58  * | 00 |
59  * | 0 |
60  * +------------------------+
61  *
62  * Another possible collection of clusters built from the source:
63  *
64  * +------------------------+
65  * | 0 | Legend: 0 : hits belonging to cluster #0
66  * | 11 00 | 1 : hits belonging to cluster #1
67  * | 111 00000 | U : unclustered hits
68  * | 1111100 |
69  * | 1 U |
70  * | 11 U |
71  * | 11 |
72  * | 1 |
73  * +------------------------+
74  *
75  * Yet another possible collection of clusters built from the source:
76  *
77  * +------------------------+
78  * | 0 | Legend: 0 : hits belonging to cluster #0
79  * | 11 00 | 1 : hits belonging to cluster #1
80  * | 111 00000 | 2 : hits belonging to cluster #2
81  * | 1111100 | U : unclustered hits
82  * | 2 U |
83  * | 22 U |
84  * | 22 |
85  * | 2 |
86  * +------------------------+
87  *
88  */
