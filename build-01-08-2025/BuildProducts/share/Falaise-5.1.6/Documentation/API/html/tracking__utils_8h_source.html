20  /// Tracking precluster of calibrated Geiger hits after space/time partitionning
21  ///
22  /// A tracking precluster can be used as a region of interest where to search for
23  /// charged particle track clusters. Particularly, a precluster may contains several
24  /// tracks. It is the task of a tracking algorithm/driver to extract track clusters
25  /// from a precluster.
26  ///
27  /// - a precluster is located on one side of the SuperNEMO tracking chamber.
28  /// - a precluster is tagged as a 'prompt' one ot a 'delayed' one, depending on the time coincidence
29  /// window used by the preclusterizer algorithm.
30  /// - a precluster contains a collection of Geiger hits.
31  ///
32  /// In principle, after the preclusterizer algorithm has made is job, one should obtain no
33  /// more than two 'prompt' preclusters (one per side) and up to N 'delayed' clusters, depending
34  /// on the ability of the preclusterizer to disantangle several delayed time windows.
35  ///
