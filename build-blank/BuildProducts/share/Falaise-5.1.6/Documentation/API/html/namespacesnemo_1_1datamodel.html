◆ vertex_category_type

enum snemo::datamodel::vertex_category_type

Supported categories of vertex.

Enumerator
VERTEX_CATEGORY_UNDEF 

Undefined category.

VERTEX_CATEGORY_ON_SOURCE_FOIL 

Maybe a source strip or pad bulk physical volume.

VERTEX_CATEGORY_ON_MAIN_CALORIMETER 

The entrance window of a main calorimeter block (front)

VERTEX_CATEGORY_ON_X_CALORIMETER 

The entrance window of a main X-calorimeter block.

VERTEX_CATEGORY_ON_GAMMA_VETO 

The bulk volume of a gamma veto block (no way to locate the entrance window)

VERTEX_CATEGORY_ON_WIRE 

The vicinity of a wire in the tracking chamber.

VERTEX_CATEGORY_ON_CALIBRATION_SOURCE 

A calibration source (Bi207)

VERTEX_CATEGORY_ON_SOURCE_GAP 

A gap between source pads (vertex reconstructed in a region of the source plane without material !)
